# Nori vs Puro.earth: Carbon Marketplace Comparison

Two leading carbon removal marketplaces—Nori and Puro.earth—take distinctly different approaches to connecting buyers with CO₂ removal projects. This guide compares their models, pricing, and target audiences.

## Platform Overview

### Nori

Founded in 2017, Nori focuses primarily on soil carbon sequestration through regenerative agriculture in the United States. Their mission: make carbon removal accessible and transparent.

### Puro.earth

Launched in 2019, Puro.earth is a European-based marketplace offering verified carbon removal certificates (CORCs) across multiple methodologies, targeting primarily corporate buyers.

## Key Differences

| Aspect | Nori | Puro.earth |
|--------|------|------------|
| **Primary Method** | Soil carbon sequestration | Biochar, DAC, enhanced weathering |
| **Geographic Focus** | United States | Europe & Global |
| **Target Buyer** | Individuals, SMBs | Corporations |
| **Min Purchase** | 1 tonne (~$15-30) | Typically bulk orders |
| **Pricing** | Transparent, per-tonne | Contact for quotes |
| **Verification** | Nori methodology | Third-party ISO 14064-2/3 |
| **Registry** | Blockchain-based | Centralized |
| **Permanence** | 10-100 years (soil) | 100-1000+ years (varies) |

## Verification Standards

### Nori

- Uses proprietary methodology based on soil sampling and modeling
- Annual verification of enrolled fields
- Conservative discounting for uncertainty
- Transparency: all supplier farms listed publicly

**Criticism**: Some scientists question soil carbon measurement precision.

### Puro.earth

- Requires third-party verification per ISO standards
- Covers biochar, direct air capture, and enhanced weathering
- Strict additionality requirements
- Longer permanence guarantees

**Criticism**: Less accessible to smaller buyers; pricing opaque.

## Pricing & Accessibility

## Use Cases

### When to Choose Nori

- You want to offset personal or small business emissions
- Budget is $100-$1,000
- You value supporting U.S. farmers
- You prioritize accessibility over maximum permanence

### When to Choose Puro.earth

- You're a corporation with net-zero commitments
- You require ISO-verified removals
- You need higher permanence (100+ years)
- You're willing to pay premium prices

## Carbon Removal Methods

### Nori: Soil Carbon

Farmers adopt practices like cover cropping, reduced tillage, and rotational grazing. Carbon is sequestered in soil organic matter.

**Challenges**:
- Saturation limits (soil can only hold so much carbon)
- Reversibility (tillage or drought can release carbon)
- Measurement uncertainty

### Puro.earth: Engineered Removals

**Biochar**: Agricultural waste is pyrolyzed into stable carbon, then buried or used as soil amendment.

**Direct Air Capture (DAC)**: Machines chemically capture CO₂ from ambient air, compress it, and inject it underground.

**Enhanced Weathering**: Crushed minerals are spread on land to accelerate natural CO₂ mineralization.

## Tax & Reporting

- **Nori**: Provides purchase receipt; not a financial instrument
- **Puro.earth**: Issues certificate; corporate buyers may report under GHG Protocol

Neither is a regulated financial product in most jurisdictions.

## Our Take

**For individuals and small businesses**, Nori offers unmatched accessibility and transparency. The soil carbon methodology has limitations, but it's a practical entry point.

**For corporations**, Puro.earth's rigorous verification and diverse removal methods justify the premium. If your CFO requires ISO-certified removals, Puro.earth is the answer.

Both platforms are pioneering the carbon removal market. As the industry matures, expect convergence on standards and broader retail access.
