# AcreTrader vs FarmTogether: Platform Review 2025

Fractional farmland investing has opened institutional-quality agricultural real estate to accredited investors. AcreTrader and FarmTogether are the two leading platforms, but they differ significantly in structure, fees, and investor experience.

## Platform Overview

### AcreTrader

Launched in 2018, AcreTrader allows investors to buy shares in individual farms across the U.S. The platform sources properties, conducts due diligence, and manages farms through local operators.

### FarmTogether

Founded in 2018, FarmTogether offers fractional ownership in diversified farmland portfolios and single-farm offerings, with a focus on permanent crops (almonds, pistachios, wine grapes).

## Head-to-Head Comparison

| Feature | AcreTrader | FarmTogether |
|---------|------------|--------------|
| **Min Investment** | $10,000-$50,000 | $10,000-$25,000 |
| **Management Fee** | 2% annually | 1% annually |
| **Performance Fee** | 5-10% on sale | 15% above 10% IRR |
| **Crop Focus** | Row crops (corn, soy) | Permanent crops (nuts, grapes) |
| **Hold Period** | 3-10 years | 5-15 years |
| **Tax Form** | K-1 | K-1 |
| **Secondary Market** | None | None |
| **Geographic Focus** | Midwest, South | California, Pacific NW |

## Fee Structures Explained

### AcreTrader Fees

- **2% annual management fee** deducted from cash distributions
- **5-10% performance fee** on farmland appreciation at sale
- **Example**: On $20,000 invested, you'd pay $400/year + 5-10% of any gains on exit

### FarmTogether Fees

- **1% annual management fee** (lower than AcreTrader)
- **15% performance fee** above 10% IRR hurdle
- **Example**: If your investment returns 12% annualized, FarmTogether takes 15% of returns above 10%

<Callout type="info">
FarmTogether's hurdle structure can be investor-friendly if farms perform well, while AcreTrader's flat performance fee is simpler to understand.
</Callout>

## Crop Types & Risk Profile

### AcreTrader: Row Crops

Focus on annual crops like corn, soybeans, cotton, and wheat. These farms typically:

- Generate annual cash income
- Have lower capital intensity
- Face commodity price volatility
- Benefit from crop insurance

**Risk**: Commodity prices, weather, input costs

### FarmTogether: Permanent Crops

Focus on orchards and vineyards (almonds, pistachios, wine grapes). These farms:

- Require years to mature
- Generate little income initially
- Have higher capital requirements
- Produce for 20-40 years once mature

**Risk**: Longer payback, water availability, market demand

## Historical Returns

<Callout type="warning">
Both platforms are young (launched 2018), so long-term track record is limited. Most farms have not yet been sold, making actual IRR data incomplete.
</Callout>

**AcreTrader** reports 6-10% target annual cash yield plus 3-5% expected appreciation.

**FarmTogether** targets 8-12% net IRR, with lower initial cash distributions but stronger appreciation potential.

## Investor Experience

### AcreTrader

- **Deal Flow**: Frequent offerings (multiple per month)
- **Due Diligence**: Detailed farm reports with soil maps, yield history
- **Transparency**: Strong; all offerings visible to members
- **Communication**: Quarterly updates per farm

### FarmTogether

- **Deal Flow**: Selective (fewer, larger farms)
- **Due Diligence**: In-depth focus on permanent crop economics
- **Transparency**: Good; property videos and financial models
- **Communication**: Quarterly reporting with detailed performance metrics

## Liquidity & Exit

Both platforms are **highly illiquid**:

- No secondary market
- Typical hold periods: 3-15 years
- Exit triggered by platform's sale of underlying farm
- No guarantee of liquidity

<Callout type="danger" title="Illiquidity Risk">
Only invest capital you won't need for 5-10+ years. These are not liquid investments.
</Callout>

## Tax Considerations

Both platforms issue **K-1 forms**, which:

- Are more complex than 1099s
- May delay your tax filing
- Include items like depreciation and recapture
- Require accountant familiarity

## Which Platform Is Right for You?

### Choose AcreTrader If:

- You prefer row crops with annual cash flow
- You want more deal flow and diversification options
- You're comfortable with slightly higher fees
- You prefer Midwest/Southern farmland

### Choose FarmTogether If:

- You're attracted to permanent crop economics
- You prioritize lower management fees
- You have a longer investment horizon (10+ years)
- You believe in California ag despite water challenges

## Our Take

Both platforms democratize access to institutional farmland. **AcreTrader** offers broader deal flow and simpler row crop economics, while **FarmTogether** focuses on permanent crops with stronger long-term appreciation potential.

For diversification, consider investing in both if you meet the minimums. Farmland's low correlation to stocks makes it a compelling portfolio diversifier, regardless of platform.
