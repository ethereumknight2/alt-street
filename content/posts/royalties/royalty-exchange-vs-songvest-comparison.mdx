# Royalty Exchange vs SongVest: Music Royalty Platform Comparison

Music royalties offer a unique alternative income stream backed by intellectual property. Royalty Exchange and SongVest are the two primary platforms for retail investors, but their models differ significantly.

## Platform Models

### Royalty Exchange: Auction Marketplace

Royalty Exchange operates as an eBay-style auction where artists, songwriters, and producers list their royalty streams for sale. Investors bid competitively.

<AffiliateButton id="royalty-exchange" />

**Pros**: Wide selection, transparent pricing discovery, full ownership transfer

**Cons**: Requires active bidding, due diligence burden on buyers, winner's curse risk

### SongVest: Fractional Shares

SongVest offers fractional ownership in professionally curated song catalogs. The platform selects songs, conducts due diligence, and sells shares to multiple investors.

<AffiliateButton id="songvest" />

**Pros**: Professional curation, diversified exposure, no bidding wars

**Cons**: Less selection, higher platform fees, shared ownership

## Investment Minimums & Fees

| Feature | Royalty Exchange | SongVest |
|---------|------------------|----------|
| **Min Investment** | Varies ($500-$100k+) | $1,000 per offering |
| **Buyer's Premium** | 10% | 5-10% |
| **Ongoing Fees** | None | 15% of royalties collected |
| **Ownership** | 100% of listed rights | Fractional % |

## How Royalty Income Works

Both platforms provide exposure to **mechanical, performance, and sync royalties**:

- **Mechanical**: Streaming (Spotify, Apple Music) and physical sales
- **Performance**: Radio, live venues, public performance (via PROs like ASCAP, BMI)
- **Sync**: TV, film, commercials, video games

<Callout type="info">
You do NOT own the copyright or master recording—only the right to receive royalty payments for a defined term or in perpetuity.
</Callout>

## Typical Deal Structures

### Royalty Exchange Auctions

- **Average Sale Price**: $5,000-$50,000 (varies widely)
- **Payment Structure**: Lump sum upfront to seller
- **Term**: 10-20 years or perpetuity
- **Due Diligence**: Buyer's responsibility; platform provides earnings history (typically 2-3 years)

**Example Auction**:
> *Country songwriter offers 10-year royalty stream. Last 3 years averaged $8,000/year. Winning bid: $60,000 (7.5x multiple).*

### SongVest Offerings

- **Offering Size**: $50,000-$500,000 total raise
- **Min Investment**: $1,000
- **Term**: Perpetuity or long-term (20+ years)
- **Curation**: Professional selection with hit potential

**Example Offering**:
> *Multi-platinum pop song generating $120,000/year in royalties. SongVest offers 50% fractional ownership to investors.*

## Returns & Risk

<Callout type="warning">
Past royalty performance does NOT guarantee future income. Streaming rates, radio play, and cultural relevance shift unpredictably.
</Callout>

**Best Case**: Catalog continues generating stable income; you earn 10-15% annualized cash yield

**Worst Case**: Song falls out of favor; royalties decline 50-80%; you lose principal

**Realistic**: Income decays 2-5% annually as catalog ages; you break even or earn modest returns

## Tax Treatment

- **Income Type**: Ordinary income (not qualified dividends)
- **Tax Form**: 1099-MISC
- **Deductions**: May amortize purchase price over royalty term (consult CPA)

Unlike stock dividends, music royalties are taxed as ordinary income at your marginal rate.

## Due Diligence Checklist

Before investing in music royalties:

1. **Verify Earnings History**: Request 3+ years of royalty statements
2. **Check Rights Clarity**: Confirm ownership and absence of disputes
3. **Assess Catalog Durability**: Evaluate genre, artist longevity, and streaming trends
4. **Model Decay**: Assume 3-5% annual decline unless exceptional catalog
5. **Understand Recoupment**: Some deals include unrecouped advances

## Which Platform to Choose?

**Choose Royalty Exchange** if:
- You have specific genre or artist preferences
- You enjoy deal evaluation and bidding
- You want 100% ownership
- You're comfortable with self-directed due diligence

**Choose SongVest** if:
- You prefer professional curation
- You want smaller investment minimums
- You value diversified fractional exposure
- You trust the platform's selection process

## Final Thoughts

Music royalties are speculative income investments, not growth assets. They're best suited for investors seeking uncorrelated cash flow who understand entertainment IP.

Both platforms have facilitated millions in transactions, but investor outcomes vary widely. Approach with realistic expectations and never invest more than 5-10% of your alternative allocation.
