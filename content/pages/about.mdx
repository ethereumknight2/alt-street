# About AltStreet

AltStreet is your trusted source for alternative investment research and analysis. We cover emerging asset classes that offer diversification beyond traditional stocks and bonds.

## Our Mission

We believe that sophisticated investment opportunities should be accessible to everyone, not just institutions and high-net-worth individuals. Our mission is to educate, inform, and empower retail investors to make confident decisions in alternative markets.

## What We Cover

### Carbon Markets
Voluntary carbon offsets, carbon removal credits, and climate-focused investments through platforms like Nori, Puro.earth, and emerging carbon registries.

### AI Compute
Decentralized GPU networks, render tokens, and infrastructure plays in the AI compute stack including platforms like Render Network and Akash.

### Fractional Farmland
Direct farmland investing through platforms like AcreTrader, FarmTogether, and agricultural REITs that allow diversification into productive land.

### Royalties
Music, film, and intellectual property royalty streams via Royalty Exchange, SongVest, and other IP investment platforms.

### Collectibles
Fractional art through Masterworks, fine wine via Vinovest, whisky casks, classic cars, watches, and other passion assets.

## Our Approach

We take a research-first approach:

- **Data-Driven**: We analyze platform fees, historical returns, liquidity terms, and tax implications
- **Transparent**: We clearly disclose affiliate relationships and potential conflicts of interest
- **Educational**: We explain complex concepts in accessible language
- **Independent**: Our analysis is based on publicly available information and direct platform research

## Editorial Standards

- All articles are thoroughly researched and fact-checked
- We disclose affiliate relationships on every applicable page
- We never recommend investments we wouldn't consider ourselves
- We update content regularly to reflect market changes

## Affiliate Disclosure

AltStreet participates in affiliate programs with some platforms we review. When you sign up through our links, we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. These relationships never influence our editorial judgment—we only feature platforms we believe merit coverage.
