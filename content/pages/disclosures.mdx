# Affiliate Disclosures

**Last Updated: November 10, 2025**

## Affiliate Relationships

AltStreet participates in affiliate marketing programs. This means that when you click certain links on our site and subsequently open an account or make a purchase, we may receive a commission from that platform or service provider.

### How It Works

1. You click an affiliate link on AltStreet
2. You're directed to the partner platform's website
3. You create an account, invest, or make a purchase
4. The partner platform pays us a commission (at no additional cost to you)

### Which Links Are Affiliate Links?

We mark affiliate content in several ways:

- Articles containing affiliate links display a disclosure banner at the top
- Individual affiliate buttons include disclosure text
- Platform review pages clearly state when a link is an affiliate link
- All affiliate links include `rel="sponsored nofollow"` attributes

## Editorial Independence

Our affiliate relationships **do not influence our editorial judgment**. Here's our commitment:

### We Only Cover Quality Platforms

We feature platforms based on:
- Market relevance and user base

- Reasonable fee structures

- Track record and reputation

### Honest Pros & Cons

Every platform review includes:
- Transparent fee breakdowns

### Updates & Corrections

We regularly review and update our content to reflect:

If we make an error, we correct it promptly and transparently.

## Legal Disclaimer

### Not Financial Advice

AltStreet is an educational resource. Nothing on this site constitutes financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Always:

- Do your own research
- Consult qualified financial, legal, and tax professionals
- Read platform disclosures and offering documents
- Understand the risks before investing

### Investment Risks

Alternative investments carry significant risks including:

- **Illiquidity**: Many alternatives have long lock-up periods or limited secondary markets
- **Concentration**: Fractional ownership may not provide adequate diversification
- **Complexity**: Tax reporting (K-1s) and valuation can be complex
- **Loss of Capital**: You may lose some or all of your investment
- **Regulatory**: Regulatory frameworks for some alternatives remain evolving

### Not an Endorsement

Featuring a platform on AltStreet does not constitute an endorsement or recommendation. We present information; you make decisions.

### Platform Relationships

Our affiliate relationships are with platforms, not individual investments. We do not receive compensation based on investment performance.
