# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 10, 2025**

## Introduction

AltStreet ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Email Address**: When you subscribe to our newsletter
- **Communication**: When you contact us via email

### Automatically Collected Information

- **Analytics**: We use Plausible Analytics, a privacy-friendly analytics service
- **Cookies**: Minimal cookies for essential site functionality
- **Device Information**: Browser type, device type, IP address (anonymized)

## How We Use Your Information

### Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter:
- We store your email address with our newsletter provider (ConvertKit or Beehiiv)
- We send you periodic emails about alternative investment topics
- You can unsubscribe anytime via the link in every email

### Analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to understand:
- How many people visit our site
- Which articles are popular
- Where traffic comes from
- General geographic regions (country-level only)

**Plausible is privacy-friendly**: It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across sites, and doesn't collect personal data. Read [Plausible's data policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy).

### Affiliate Tracking

When you click an affiliate link:
- The destination platform may set cookies
- The platform may track that you came from AltStreet
- We have no control over their tracking practices
- Review each platform's privacy policy before signing up

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights including:

### Right to Access
Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

### Right to Deletion
Request deletion of your personal information.

### Right to Unsubscribe
Unsubscribe from our newsletter anytime via the unsubscribe link.

### Right to Object
Object to processing of your personal information.

## Data Retention

- **Newsletter**: We retain email addresses until you unsubscribe
- **Analytics**: Plausible retains anonymized data per their policy
- **Correspondence**: We retain emails for record-keeping purposes

## Children's Privacy

AltStreet is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## International Visitors

AltStreet is based in the United States. If you visit from outside the U.S., your information may be transferred to and processed in the U.S.

## Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
